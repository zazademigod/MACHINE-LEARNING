					DECISION TREES
decision trees are the basic building blocks for some of the best models for data science.
lets look at a basic decision tree, A basic scenario of a question about a house and the response affect the price 
		"does the house have more than two bedrooms?'
	(NO)							(YES)
PREDICTED PRICE :$178000				PREDICTED PRICE: $188,000


the above example allows us see how a basic decision tree works, the answer gives a deecision that impacts the price.

in the house example, it divides houses into 2 categories, the predicted price for any house under consideration is the historical aerage price od houses in the same category.

The step of capturing patterns from data is called fitting or training the model.
The data used to fit the model is called the Training data.

IMPROVING DECISION TREES
To capture more factors in the decision tree, we use a tee that has more splits. These are called "deeper trees".

to access a decision tree, we folow the characteristic/factor we want down the tee, the point at the bottom is where we make a prediction and it's called a leaf.
